# hat-stand-male-thread-test specification

## Requirements

- [overall-size] Overall dimensions are mesh-verified.
- [thread-test] The 99%-diameter Tr24x3 male thread is source-verified.
- [coupon-shape] Shape and orientation are visually verified in all ten views.

## Exclusions

THR-008 records this pair as very loose but functional when fully bottomed out. It is accepted for the hat stand only and should not be reused where partial engagement must hold position. Slicing, material, nozzle, support, overhang, and toolpath assessment are out of scope.
