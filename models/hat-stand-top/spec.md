# hat-stand-top specification

## Requirements

- [overall-size] Overall dimensions are mesh-verified.
- [top-parameters] Perch, socket, and clearance relationships are source-verified.
- [female-thread] The centered female Tr24x3 thread is source-verified.
- [top-shape] The circular plate, cylindrical column, and top-side female socket are visually verified in all ten views.

## Exclusions

Physical trial THR-008 found the matching Tr24x3 pair very loose, but functional when fully bottomed out. That fit is accepted only for this hat stand's fully tightened joint and is not suitable evidence for partial-engagement positioning. Slicing, material, nozzle, support, overhang, and toolpath assessment are out of scope.
