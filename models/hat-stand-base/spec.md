# hat-stand-base specification

## Requirements

- [overall-size] Overall dimensions are mesh-verified.
- [base-parameters] Foot, post, and overlap relationships are source-verified.
- [male-thread] The centered male Tr24x3 thread is source-verified.
- [base-shape] The circular plate and cylindrical column are visually verified in all ten views.

## Exclusions

Physical trial THR-008 found this 99%-diameter Tr24x3 male thread very loose in the matching 1.2 mm-clearance socket, but functional when fully bottomed out. That fit is accepted only for this hat stand's fully tightened joint and is not suitable evidence for partial-engagement positioning. Slicing, material, nozzle, support, overhang, and toolpath assessment are out of scope.
