# Soft tapered planter specification

## Requirements

- [overall-size] The planter is exactly 168 mm across the scallop tips and 145 mm tall, mesh-verified.
- [planter-parameters] The 126 mm base, outward taper, 3 mm side wall, and 4 mm floor are source-verified.
- [drainage-pattern] The floor has one central and four equally spaced surrounding 8 mm drainage holes on a 48 mm pitch circle, source-verified.
- [scalloped-rim] The entire top perimeter has twelve evenly spaced lobes that begin 48.33 mm below the top, fade in with a smooth eased transition, and reach tips 4 mm beyond the original 160 mm circular rim, source-verified.
- [open-center-drain] The central drainage opening passes through the floor, mesh-verified.
- [intended-shape] The smooth tapered vessel, open top, softened transitions, and clean drainage features are visually verified in all ten views.

## Exclusions

Fabricated watertightness, runoff performance, stability, slicing, material, nozzle, support, overhang, and toolpath assessment require downstream or physical validation.
