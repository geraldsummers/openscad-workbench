# BOSL2 threaded bolt specification

## Requirements

- [overall-size] Overall dimensions are approximately 32 x 27.713 x 37 mm: mesh-verified by `model.toml`.
- [thread-parameters] The BOSL2 external thread has an 18 mm major diameter, 3 mm pitch, at least six pitches, and a connected overlap with its head: source-verified by OpenSCAD assertions and a pass marker.
- [open-thread-profile] A continuous right-handed external helical thread is formed along the shaft: visually verified in the four elevation and two isometric views.
- [intended-shape] The result is one upright threaded bolt demonstrator with a chamfered hex head, connected shaft, and beveled tip: visually verified in all eight views.

## Exclusions

This is an oversized thread demonstrator, not a standards-certified mating fastener. Fit and clearance against a nut remain unverified. Slicing, material, nozzle, support, overhang, and toolpath assessment are out of scope.
